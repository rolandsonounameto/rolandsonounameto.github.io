Malaria · Modelling · Impact evaluation

Evidence for better malaria control decisions.

I am a PhD fellow in Biometry (University of Abomey-Calavi, AIMS-RIC and Swiss TPH) and a Gates Scholar in the MaModAfrica programme. I evaluate malaria interventions in Benin with statistical and agent-based models, and help national malaria programmes use modelling results in their decisions.

Selected projects

  • PhDBenin · 2019–2022 data

    Effectiveness of seasonal malaria chemoprevention in northern Benin

    Synthetic control design and negative binomial mixed-effects models on routine surveillance data.

  • PhDOpenMalaria · 8 trials

    Joint parameterisation of SMC protective decay

    A Weibull decay function calibrated across eight randomised trials. It shows that coverage and delivery timing, rather than drug decay, drive the gap between trial efficacy and real-world effectiveness.

  • PhDBenin · ongoing

    Prioritising malaria interventions under budget constraints

    OpenMalaria calibrated to Benin's health districts, used to rank intervention packages by cost-effectiveness for the national malaria programme.
